# Domain glossary (English / Arabic)

- **Task:** T-DG0-AN-01 (transformation-analyst), stage P0/DG0.
- **Source:** `docs/source/playbook.md` v1.0.

Definitions paraphrase or quote the playbook (block IDs given). The Arabic terms are *proposed* Modern Standard Arabic equivalents in Saudi business usage. They need review by a Mobily Arabic-language owner before release, because no official Mobily terminology list was supplied. Acronyms that are commonly kept in Latin script in Saudi corporate usage (KPI, RACI, RAID, SLA, BAU, TOM) are shown with an Arabic expansion; the UI may display the acronym alongside it.

The playbook is a practical synthesis *inspired by* PMI/Brightline/BRM with custom extensions (B0004). No term here implies official PMI status.

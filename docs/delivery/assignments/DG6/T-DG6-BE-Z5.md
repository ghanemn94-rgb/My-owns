This is the third round in which a different read path leaks a sensitive benefit (F-DG6-180, F-DG6-220, now these two). Fixing only the named routes again is not acceptable. Fix the class.

1. **F-DG6-300 (High, mandatory, REQ-S10-002): derived views carry the benefit's code, title and values.** A user who gets 404 on a sensitive benefit still sees its id, code, title and values through:
   - `getTraceability` (graph node label and href);
   - `missing-links` (item label);
   - the G6 gate readiness text (missing item "Benefits evidence: B01 <title> …");
   - the live register report (`reports/registers/live`, register `benefits`), and the generated snapshot (`reports/registers/generate`, then `report-snapshots/{id}`), including planned, validated, sustained and submitted amounts.
   Apply ADR-0051 §2 (as amended on 2026-11-04) on each of these: omit the restricted record, with no count leak, or, where a count or readiness verdict must stay truthful, show a neutral placeholder with no code, title or value (say which, and why, per route). A stored snapshot generated by an admitted user must not show restricted rows to a reader who is not admitted.
2. **F-DG6-280 (High, mandatory): the escalation copies the benefit's title into an executive ask.** The worker's corrective consumer titles a `benefit_variance` case with `benefit.title` (`apps/worker/src/handlers/raid.ts` around line 535). `handleBlockerEscalation` (`apps/worker/src/handlers/escalations.ts`) then copies that title into an executive ask and its notification, which readers who are not admitted can see through `GET /executive-decisions`, `GET /executive-decisions/{askId}` and `GET /me/inbox`. Fix both ends: derived records must not copy a sensitive record's title or values into a record readers outside the grant can see (title the case and the ask neutrally, for example by code, or apply the restriction to the derived ask). Also judge whether a user who gets 404 on the case may record a blocker status that names it, and either refuse it (404) or explain why it is safe.
3. **The class: a sweep test.** Add a product integration test that, as an admitted reader and as a reader who is not admitted, calls every GET operation in `docs/api/openapi.yaml` that can be called with the seeded ids (as the code-security probe `zz-cs-r4-sens-sweep.test.ts` does over the 128 `{transformationId}` operations and the live reports), plus the report-snapshot, inbox and executive-decision reads, and asserts that the sensitive benefit's title, code-with-title and distinctive planned value never appear in a response to the reader who is not admitted. The admitted reader must still see them (positive control). Port the reviewers' probes into product tests that fail before the fix.
4. **Separable second half (D-059/D-070):** if you run out of time, finish item 1 and item 3 first; item 2 is the separable second half.

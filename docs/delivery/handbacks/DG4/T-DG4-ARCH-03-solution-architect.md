## 6. Known gaps, interpretations and decisions for the orchestrator

1. **REQ-PB-058 enforcement points (interpretation).** One owner and the statement line or KPI are enforced at create. Baseline, formula and target are enforced before Enable/Measure and before any value can exist, because REQ-PB-074 makes them Plan outputs (ADR-0029 §1). Reviewers should test both points.
2. **KBE-D2 split (recommendation).** Slice B has 45 operations. I propose a third task, KBE-D2 (13 operations: overlaps, scenarios, valuation methods), between KBE-D and KBE-E. If it is not scheduled, KBE-D owns its files and `p4-pending-kbe-d2.ts` as a second half (p4-work-split §B.2).
3. **Validated vs sustained.** Per M0166 ("separately"), values validated after the benefit entered Sustain form the `sustained` series and are not in the `validated` line. An A10 test that validates a value of a Sustain-step benefit sees `sustained` increase, not `validated`.
4. **Finance corrections.** An amendment or reversal is a Finance-authored record, approved on creation by its author (named in both rows). It has no second Finance reviewer in DG4.
5. **Queue item timing.** The queue item is created by the `benefits.finance_queue` consumer of `benefit.evidence_submitted`, which makes it eventually consistent with the submit. This is the REQ-S12-014 starter-automation shape, and its rule-builder configurability is DG5's (D-089 R5).
6. **S07-014 binding.** One KPI-fed formula variable per benefit (`measurement_kpi_variable`). Multi-KPI binding is not in DG4.
7. **Contract paths** use the transformation-scoped convention (e.g. `POST …/finance-validations/{id}/decision`), not the requirement notes' hint paths `/api/v1/validations` and `/api/v1/finance-validations`; the acceptance texts do not test paths.
8. **Untracked files not mine:** top-level `.bash_profile`, `.bashrc`, `.gitconfig`, `.gitmodules`, `.idea`, `.mcp.json`, `.profile`, `.ripgreprc`, `.vscode`, `.zprofile`, `.zshrc`, `CLAUDE.local.md` were present or created by the environment; I did not create or edit them.

## 8. What the implementers of slice B must know

See p4-work-split §B.8. In short:

- totals sum canonical benefits (`benefit_counting.counted`), never allocations, group members or parents;
- Plan outputs gate Enable/Measure;
- pending is never validated, and only an all-items-accepted Finance decision validates;
- validated rows are immutable (409) and are corrected by linked amendment/reversal rows;
- exactly one queue item, through `runOnce` and the idempotency key;
- the basis (baseline, and the formula version when one is used) is Finance-validated first;
- currencies are never converted;
- decimal.js only;
- the Finance endpoints return 403 for BO, AUD and ADM-only callers;
- the formula engine is unchanged.

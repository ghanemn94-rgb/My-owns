Gate exceptions
An exception lets one mandatory criterion be submitted incomplete. It needs a reason, a scope, a compensating action with an owner and an expiry date, and it is decided by the gate's configured approver. It covers the criterion only while today is on or before its expiry date; after that the criterion is missing again.

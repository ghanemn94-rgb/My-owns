| D-016 | 2026-09-28 | delivery-orchestrator | **Provenance binding** (F-DG0-102, F-DG0-201). The validator accepts a review, audit or finding verification only when its run:<br>• has `meta.json`, `result.json` and `transcript.jsonl.gz`, with SHA-256 hashes recorded in meta that match;<br>• has an init line, a runner prompt (role and run ID) and a final successful result line for the same session in its transcript;<br>• has a requested model that matches the transcript;<br>• has the same stage and role as the record;<br>• executed exactly the assignment the record cites (path and SHA-256);<br>• started after the candidate froze. | The runner's evidence is verified, not trusted by its label. | D-017 | 2026-09-28 | delivery-orchestrator | **Approval immutability** (F-DG0-102). Changing them requires rewriting published git history. | It stops approvals from being rewritten after the fact. CI checks out full history (`fetch-depth: 0`). | — |
| D-018 | 2026-09-28 | delivery-orchestrator | **Findings integrity** (F-DG0-101). The fields the reviewer sets (stage, requirement, severity, mandatory flag, title, reporter) can't be altered there. Findings can't be relabelled to another stage. Only Low, non-mandatory findings can be accepted as observations, and that needs a specialist reviewer and the auditor. | This stops unresolved findings escaping a gate by being dropped, relabelled or downgraded. | — |

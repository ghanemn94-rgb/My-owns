roaming.user.tl (synthetic) (Transformation Lead) · you
Case for change
Roaming has value leakage from low adoption, fragmented propositions, inconsistent digital activation and avoidable CX friction.
North Star
Make international roaming effortless, trusted and economically accretive.
Current Current since 14 May 2026, 09:00
Top outcomes
#1 Attach rate / activation
#2 Complaint rate
#3 Cost/MB, margin
In scope
Outcomes: Revenue growth; higher attach/activation; improved partner economics; fewer complaints; higher digital self-service. Initiatives: Pass architecture redesign; digital activation; proactive travel triggers; partner-cost optimization; QoS monitoring; care simplification; analytics.
Out of scope
Domestic tariffs and non-roaming propositions (synthetic illustrative example).
Baseline date
1 Jun 2026
Target horizon
18 months
Strategic guardrails
No bill shock (synthetic) (Customer experience)
Governance forum
Monthly executive review; bi-weekly transformation review; weekly workstreams; monthly value review with Finance.
Decision rights
Per the T11 decision rights matrix of this transformation (synthetic).
Success definition
Benefits: Incremental roaming revenue, margin improvement, complaint reduction, digital adoption, avoided care cost.
Transformation thesis
Complete the sentence (B0037): If we change [capabilities / journeys / operating model], then [customer/operational outcomes] will improve, which will create [financial/strategic benefits], because [evidence / causal logic].
If we change the roaming pass architecture, the digital activation journey and the partner model, then roaming attach and activation, complaint rate and partner economics will improve, which will create incremental roaming revenue, avoided care cost and margin improvement, because low activation, high complaints and partner cost pressure are the diagnosed causes of roaming value leakage (synthetic).

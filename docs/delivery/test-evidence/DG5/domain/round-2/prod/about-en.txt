Provisional branding

No official Mobily logo or verified brand colours have been supplied. The header shows a provisional text wordmark, and the colours (including #0078FF) are provisional design tokens that can be replaced through Branding Settings once official values are approved.

Arabic and Latin text use IBM Plex Sans Arabic and IBM Plex Sans (SIL Open Font License 1.1), bundled with the application. No public font service is used.

Methodology

The product operationalizes the Business Transformation Playbook v1.0, a practical synthesis inspired by PMI, Brightline and BRM practice with custom extensions. It is not an official PMI standard, and this product is not certified by PMI or any other body.

Provenance statement

Practical synthesis inspired by PMI Organizational Transformation, Brightline Transformation Compass and PMI Benefits Realization Management; extended with a custom Target Operating Model toolkit.

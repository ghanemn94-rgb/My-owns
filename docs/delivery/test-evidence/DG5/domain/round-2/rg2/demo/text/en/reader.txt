About this methodology
A practical synthesis inspired by PMI and Brightline foundations, with custom extensions. It is not an official PMI standard.

Methodology version: playbook v1

Provenance statement

Practical synthesis inspired by PMI Organizational Transformation, Brightline Transformation Compass and PMI Benefits Realization Management; extended with a custom Target Operating Model toolkit.

 Playbook block B0004

PMI/Brightline connection
Playbook block B0024
PMI/Brightline connection The playbook uses the people-centered transformation logic and transformation operating-system thinking associated with PMI/Brightline, while adding a more explicit Target Operating Model and management-consulting-style diagnostic/design layer.

 Playbook block B0024

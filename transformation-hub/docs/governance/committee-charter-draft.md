## 1. Purpose (الغرض)

The DC Carve-out & JV Steering Committee (the **Committee**, `اللجنة التوجيهية`) oversees the data center carve-out
program end to end: separating the business, establishing and preparing a standalone NewCo (`الشركة الجديدة`), and
progressing to a joint venture (`المشروع المشترك`). It provides direction, takes decisions within its delegated
authority, recommends decisions that are beyond its authority to the competent body, and monitors delivery, risks,
readiness, conditions and benefits.

## 2. Scope (النطاق)

In scope, subject to the approved perimeter and authority matrix:

1. Program mandate execution, integrated plan, baseline and change control.
2. Transaction perimeter definition and changes (Included / Excluded / Shared / Pending).
3. Business gates G0–G7 (see `business-gates.md`) — assessment oversight and gate decisions within delegation.
4. Separation, Day-1 readiness, cutover go/no-go, standalone acceptance, transitional service agreements (TSAs) and exits.
5. Partner process oversight (outreach approval, materials access, diligence, negotiation positions) within delegation.
6. JV signing readiness, conditions precedent (CPs) monitoring, closing readiness, conditions subsequent (CS) and the
   100-day plan.
7. Program RAID, escalations, reporting and benefits tracking.

## 3. Relationship to other bodies (العلاقة بالجهات الأخرى)

The Committee is a **program governance body**. It is distinct from, and does not act as, any corporate body.

| Body | Nature | Relationship to the Committee |
|---|---|---|
| Delegating authority (e.g. Board of Directors or executive authority — to be confirmed) | Corporate authority that delegates to the Committee | Approves this charter and the authority matrix; decides reserved matters and anything beyond delegation |
| **DC Carve-out & JV Steering Committee** | Program governance body | Decides within delegation; recommends beyond it |
| NewCo board of directors (`مجلس إدارة الشركة الجديدة`) | Statutory body of the NewCo legal entity | Separate. Committee decisions do not bind NewCo; NewCo resolutions are recorded as evidence where relevant |
| JV board (`مجلس إدارة المشروع المشترك`) | Statutory body of the JV entity after closing | Separate. Governs the JV under the JV / shareholders' agreement |
| Functional approvers (Finance, Legal, Regulatory, Cybersecurity, etc.) | Specialist authorities | Provide specialist assessments, sign-offs and determinations of applicability and waivability |

The platform models these as separate bodies (committee vs. legal entity boards) so that a Committee decision is never
displayed as a NewCo or JV board resolution.

## 4. Delegated authority (الصلاحيات المفوضة)

1. The Committee acts only within the authority granted in the **approved** authority matrix (decision types, limits,
   currencies/units, conditions, validity).
2. A Committee resolution on a matter outside its delegation is recorded as **Recommended — pending external
   authority** (`توصية — بانتظار الجهة المختصة`) and does not count as final approval. Any gate or CP depending on it stays
   blocked until the competent body's decision is recorded with evidence.
3. Delegation has a validity period. When delegation expires or membership changes, historical votes and decisions
   remain unchanged; new decisions are evaluated against the authority valid at the time of the vote.
4. Electronic approvals in the platform are internal approval records. They are not represented as legally certified
   signatures unless an approved e-signature solution is integrated and its requirements verified.

## 5. Exclusions (الاستثناءات)

The Committee does not:

- make legal, tax, zakat, accounting or regulatory determinations (these are made by authorized specialists and recorded
  as evidence);
- approve financial statements, balances or valuations without documented human financial validation by the authorized
  finance approver;
- execute payments, sign agreements on behalf of any entity without signing authority, or control data center devices,
  networks or power;
- waive a condition that authorized specialists have classified as non-waivable;
- act as the NewCo board or the JV board.

## 6. Matters reserved for higher authorities (المسائل المحجوزة)

Proposed list — to be confirmed against the corporate delegation of authority. Final list is set in the approved matrix.

- Approval of the transaction (sale, contribution or JV formation) and of valuation and ownership terms.
- Selection of the preferred partner, authorization to sign, and confirmation of closing where beyond delegation.
- Commitments or budget changes above the Committee's approved limits.
- Changes to this charter or to the Committee's delegated authority.
- Any matter that the corporate delegation of authority reserves to the Board or executive authority.

## 9. Membership terms (شروط العضوية)

1. Members are appointed by role through an authorized appointment record; the platform stores the appointment
   reference, effective-from and effective-to dates.
2. Proposed term: for the duration of the program, subject to review at each gate decision or on organizational change.
3. A member who changes role is replaced by the new role holder through a new appointment record; the previous
   appointment is closed, not overwritten.
4. Historical attendance, votes and recusals are immutable and remain attributed to the member who cast them.

## 10. Quorum (النصاب)

1. Quorum rules are taken from the approved authority matrix. A clearly labelled **Demo** policy exists for the sandbox
   only (see `authority-matrix.md`).
2. Quorum is evaluated per agenda item: members recused from an item are excluded from the quorum count for that item.
   The fraction of the quorum rule is taken over the **appointed** voting members — voting seats held by a named person on
   the meeting date; a vacant seat ("Role — To be confirmed") is not counted. Recused members and the requester stay in
   this denominator, so recusals never lower the bar (proposed platform rule, `authority-matrix.md` §3 step 4 — A-42 / Q-42).
3. Advisory members and guests are not counted.
4. The platform rejects (server-side) and logs any attempt to record a decision without quorum.

## 11. Voting (التصويت)

1. Each voting member present and not recused has one vote: approve, reject or abstain.
2. The approval threshold (e.g. simple majority or two-thirds) is taken from the approved authority matrix. It is
   measured over the eligible votes cast (approve, reject and abstain); **abstentions count as not approving**; a round
   with abstentions only records no outcome (`authority-matrix.md` §3 step 5 — rule to be confirmed, A-40 / Q-40).
3. The requester of a decision or waiver, and the owner of evidence under decision, may not approve their own item
   (self-approval prohibited); they may present and answer questions.
4. Votes are recorded against a frozen version of the decision paper. If the paper changes after voting opens, prior
   votes are invalidated and a new vote is required.

## 12. Ties (التعادل)

The tie rule is set in the approved authority matrix: either a casting vote by the chair, or escalation to the
delegating authority. No tie rule is assumed in this draft. The Demo policy uses escalation. Where a casting vote applies,
the platform reads it as "the side the chair voted for prevails" — the chair has no second vote, and a chair who abstained,
did not vote or is not eligible for the item (recused, requester) cannot break the tie, which is then escalated
(`authority-matrix.md` §3 step 6 — A-41 / Q-41).

## 13. Delegation and alternates (الإنابة)

1. A voting member may nominate an alternate only if the approved authority matrix permits it; the alternate must hold
   an appropriate role and be recorded before the meeting.
2. Proxy voting (voting on behalf of another member) is not permitted unless expressly allowed by the approved matrix.
3. An alternate is subject to the same conflict and recusal rules.

## 14. Conflicts of interest and recusals (تعارض المصالح والتنحي)

1. Members declare conflicts at appointment and at the start of each meeting, per agenda item.
2. A conflicted member is recused from the item: no vote, not counted in quorum for that item, and (where the chair
   rules) no participation in deliberation. The recusal is recorded in the minutes.
3. Partner-related conflicts are also recorded in the partner workspace conflict disclosures.
4. A vote cast by a recused member is rejected by the server and logged.
5. A recusal is recorded **before** the member votes. Once a member has voted in the current round, a recusal for that
   member is refused (it would discard a cast vote and could change the outcome); a conflict discovered after voting is
   handled by deferring and resuming the item, which opens a new round in which the member is recused before voting.
6. A recusal recorded by the secretariat on behalf of a member requires a reason; the recorder is kept with the recusal,
   written to the audit trail and shown in the tally snapshot.

## 15. Resolutions by circulation (القرارات بالتمرير)

1. Permitted for items the chair designates as suitable, excluding reserved matters (proposed rule).
2. A circulated resolution includes the frozen decision paper and a response deadline; the circulation record shows
   recipients, responses and timestamps.
3. The same quorum (as responses received), threshold, recusal and self-approval rules apply.
4. Any member may request that an item be taken to a meeting instead; the circulation then lapses (proposed rule).
5. Circulated resolutions are tabled for noting in the next meeting's minutes.

## 16. Meetings, papers and minutes (الاجتماعات والأوراق والمحاضر)

Workflow: agenda request → secretariat screening → decision paper → agenda → quorum/conflict checks →
discussion/voting or circulation → minutes approval → actions/owners/dates → implementation tracking → verified
closure (details in `decision-workflow.md`).

Attendance is the basis of the quorum of every item voted at the meeting: it is frozen while an item has votes in its
current round and no recorded outcome; a correction after voting requires recording the outcome first or restarting the
round.

- Agendas, minutes and actions are numbered and linked to the related issue and decision.
- Meeting packs are frozen as snapshots when issued; later changes create new versions.
- Minutes are approved at the next meeting or by circulation.

## 17. Information classification and access (تصنيف المعلومات والوصول)

1. Proposed levels: Public, Internal, Confidential, Strictly Confidential (to be aligned with corporate policy).
2. Committee packs and minutes default to Confidential; partner, valuation and clean-team material may be Strictly
   Confidential with named access.
3. Members see only items and documents their role and classification permit; restricted items are not inferable from
   titles, counts or search results.
4. Exports (minutes, packs, executive summaries) respect classification and are re-authorized at download.

## 18. Minutes and records retention (الاحتفاظ بالمحاضر والسجلات)

Retention periods follow the approved corporate records policy — to be confirmed. The platform keeps minutes, packs,
votes, recusals and decision history immutable, supports legal hold, and rejects deletion under retention or hold.

## 19. Escalation (التصعيد)

Escalations to the sponsor or delegating authority state the requested action, the decision deadline (latest safe
decision date), the options with impacts, and the recommended option. Escalations and their outcomes are recorded.

## 20. Proposed cadence (الوتيرة المقترحة) — PROPOSAL, NOT A CONFIRMED SCHEDULE

| Forum | Proposed cadence | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Operational follow-up (PMO with workstream leads) | Weekly | Proposal — configurable |
| Committee meeting | Monthly, and as needed around gate decisions, Day 1, signing and closing | Proposal — configurable |
